### How to use this guide effectively?
1. Follow along with each lesson. Typing out all code in the lesson.
2. Complete all exercises before moving on to the next unit.

A few things you *SHOULDN'T* do:
1. Use AI to complete the exercises. Don't worry you'll have plenty of time to use AI in the future. You need to actually learn how stuff works first.
2. Memorize. You have the internet at your fingertips, you do not need to spend time memorizing stuff.
3. Take notes. Focus on understanding concepts and playing around with the code.
